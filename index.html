Why this exists

Weather apps say “103°F.” Crews need “15-minute cool-down every 45 minutes, hydrate 8–12 oz every 15–20 minutes, shade up, buddy check” — and a heat score that reflects humidity, sun, and radiant load, not air temp alone. Why WBGT beats air temp

Wet-bulb globe temperature folds in humidity, radiant heat (sun / steel / asphalt), and air movement — closer to what outdoor crews feel than a dry-bulb “103°F” alone. Use HeatReady triggers with GOES digital WBGT when conditions climb.
