## Why this exists

Weather apps say “103°F.” Crews need “15-minute cool-down every 45 minutes, hydrate
8–12 oz every 15–20 minutes, shade up, buddy check” — and a heat score that reflects
humidity, sun, and radiant load, not air temp alone. We publish the checklist; GOES
delivers digital WBGT and live heat-risk scores.

### By city

Phoenix, Houston, Las Vegas, California, Dallas, Miami, Tucson, Atlanta, San Antonio — climate and local context that matter on the ground.

### By industry

Construction, roofing, landscaping, utilities, agriculture, delivery — different PPE, work rates, and shade options.

### Printable & plain

Checklists, trigger tables, emergency steps. Mobile-first. Works without JavaScript. Print-friendly.

### Why WBGT beats air temp

Wet-bulb globe temperature folds in humidity, radiant heat (sun / steel / asphalt),
and air movement — closer to what outdoor crews feel than a dry-bulb “103°F” alone.
Use HeatReady triggers with GOES digital WBGT when conditions climb.
